📂 Common Documents Needed

📂 Account Opening — Common Documents Needed

These are commonly requested documents. The exact requirements can differ by bank, account type and applicant.

  • Identity proof: Aadhaar Card, Passport, Voter ID or Driving Licence.
  • Address proof: an accepted document showing your current address.
  • PAN Card: or Form 60 where applicable.
  • Passport-size photograph: if required by the bank.
  • Mobile number: an active number for verification and banking alerts.
  • Email address: where requested for communication and digital banking.
  • Additional documents: may be required for specific account types, applicants or services.
Important: Before submitting documents, verify the latest requirements on the selected bank's official website or at an authorised branch. Never share your PIN, CVV, password or OTP with this application.
